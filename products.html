Specialized Investment Funds (SIF)

SIF strategies are designed for investors looking for focused, theme-based, or strategy-led participation with professional oversight. They aim to generate alpha through conviction-led positioning rather than broad market participation.

These strategies are ideal for satellite allocations where investors seek differentiated exposure while keeping core portfolios diversified and risk-calibrated.
