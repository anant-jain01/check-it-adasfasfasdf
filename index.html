Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum investment to start?

You can start a SIP with as little as ₹500/month. For portfolio management, a minimum of ₹10 Lakhs is recommended.

How do you charge for your services?

We follow a transparent model. Mutual fund distribution is AMFI-compliant commission-based; advisory services are fee-based as discussed upfront.

Are mutual funds safe investments?

Mutual funds are market-linked and carry risk, but they are SEBI-regulated, professionally managed, and suitable for long-term wealth creation when matched to your goals.

How do I open a Demat account?

We assist you with opening a Demat account through our partner Motilal Oswal. The process is fully digital and takes less than 15 minutes.

Can NRIs invest through 3K Investment Partners?

Yes. NRIs can invest in most mutual funds and certain equity products through NRE/NRO accounts. We guide you through FEMA and RBI compliance requirements.

How often will I receive portfolio updates?

We conduct formal quarterly reviews and provide monthly market updates. You can also reach your relationship manager anytime for ad-hoc queries.
