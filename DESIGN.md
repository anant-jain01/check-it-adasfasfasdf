# Design System Document

This document outlines the core visual style of our product, ensuring consistency and coherence across all user interfaces.

## Brand Identity

Our brand identity is built on a foundation of sophisticated and natural tones, evoking a sense of heritage and grounded elegance.

### Color Palette

-   **Primary Color**: `#1B3022` - A deep, rich forest green, symbolizing growth and stability. Used for primary actions, branding, and key interactive elements.
-   **Secondary Color**: `#C5A059` - A warm, inviting gold, representing quality and heritage. Ideal for secondary actions, accents, and complementary elements.
-   **Tertiary Color**: `#2E4D36` - A muted, earthy green, providing depth and a natural feel. Used for highlights, badges, or decorative elements.
-   **Neutral Color**: `#F9F6EE` - A soft, creamy off-white, offering a clean and calming background for content.

### Typography

-   **Headlines**: `notoSerif` - Classic serif typeface, conveying gravitas and a refined aesthetic.
-   **Body Text**: `inter` - Modern sans-serif, chosen for its excellent readability and versatility across various content types.
-   **Labels**: `inter` - Consistent with body text for clarity and straightforward communication in UI elements.

### Shape and Form

Our UI elements feature **maximum, pill-shaped roundedness (3)**, contributing to a soft, approachable, and contemporary feel throughout the design.

### Spacing

We implement a **normal spacing (2)** approach, striking a balance between density and readability, ensuring a clean and organized layout without feeling overly sparse or cramped. This promotes comfortable user interaction and visual hierarchy.
